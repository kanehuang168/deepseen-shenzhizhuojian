# Minimal implementation handoff

Reference existing task/artifact authority and versions. Include observable goal and preserved behavior; exclusions; symptom evidence and facts/inferences/unknowns; competing explanations and distinguishing experiment; recommendation and scope; narrow real acceptance and previous-success regression; risks and rollback; existing authorization/budget and user-only remaining actions.

Share only what implementation needs. An unperformed experiment stays pending; complete paperwork does not establish resolution.
